Welcome to Foodwill!

Foodwill is a platform that connects people who have excess food with those in need. Our mission is to reduce food waste and help feed those who are hungry. Whether you have extra food to donate or are in need of a meal, Foodwill is here to help. Join us in our efforts to make a difference in our community and fight hunger together!
